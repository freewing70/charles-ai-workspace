# Reusable Prompt Examples

## Colossal dragon in mist

A vast silent lake disappears into endless pale mist. At the bottom center, a lone ancient Chinese wanderer in a simple black robe and woven conical hat stands on a tiny dark rock, seen from behind and extremely small in frame. Directly before him, an impossibly colossal ancient Chinese serpentine dragon slowly materializes from the fog. Its elongated head towers like a mountain; elegant antler-like horns, long thin whiskers, and a subtle mane emerge from the white haze. Only several vague sections of its enormous coiling body can be perceived before disappearing again into mist, implying that most of the creature is beyond visibility. Do not reveal the whole dragon and do not render every scale.

Traditional Chinese ink-wash inspired fantasy illustration, expressive calligraphic brushwork, diluted ink washes, dry-brush texture, feathered ink edges, subtle ink bleeding on textured xuan-paper-like surface, restrained monochrome palette, vast negative space, layered atmospheric mist, poetic cinematic scale, elegant silhouettes, selective detail, mysterious ancient mood. Hand-painted rather than digitally glossy. No Western dragon wings, no bulky monster anatomy, no game-boss composition, no photorealism, no neon effects.

## Staff-wielding wuxia hero with living ink qi

A lone Chinese wuxia warrior stands firmly on weathered stone, full body visible, wearing a dark layered robe and traditional bamboo hat. He grips a long wooden staff diagonally across his body in a calm but powerful martial stance. A tremendous invisible force awakens around him, but the energy is made entirely from living Chinese ink: black calligraphic strokes peel from his robe, smoky ink ribbons spiral around the staff, explosive dry-brush arcs sweep behind him, diluted gray washes expand outward like pressure waves, and scattered ink droplets hang in the air. The aura must feel like the painting itself is becoming alive, not like glowing magic.

Traditional Chinese ink-wash wuxia fantasy, expressive brushwork, xuan-paper texture, monochrome black and gray, strong negative space, asymmetrical composition, layered mist, restrained detail, dramatic silhouette, ancient poetic atmosphere. No fire aura, no lightning, no neon glow, no CGI particles, no superhero effects, no glossy 3D rendering, no ornate MMORPG armor.

## Wuxia duel

Two wandering martial artists face each other across a shallow mountain stream beneath towering mist-covered cliffs. One holds a straight sword low beside the body; the other carries a long staff. Their figures are small within the landscape. At the instant they move, sweeping calligraphic ink strokes describe the direction of their attacks while mist is pushed aside by invisible pressure. Keep both silhouettes readable and preserve large areas of untouched paper between the combatants and the cliffs.

Chinese ink-wash wuxia cinematic illustration, broken brush contours, dry-brush weapon arcs, diluted mountain washes, fog layers, restrained monochrome palette, xuan-paper texture, poetic tension, selective detail, large negative space. Avoid anime speed lines, glowing energy beams, photorealism, CGI, excessive effects, and crowded composition.

## Character design sheet

Design a full-body Chinese wuxia wanderer with a restrained, practical silhouette: layered weathered robe, cloth sash, simple boots, woven conical hat, and a sheathed dao. Present the character clearly against generous warm paper-white negative space. Use controlled black ink for the silhouette, dry-brush texture for cloth and leather, and diluted gray wash for secondary folds. Add two or three small gesture studies showing walking, drawing the blade, and standing in quiet readiness. Keep ornamentation minimal and functional.

Traditional Chinese ink character concept art, expressive hand-drawn contours, ink wash, dry brush, subtle bleeding, xuan-paper texture, monochrome palette, selective detail, elegant historical-fantasy mood. No glossy game concept art, no oversized armor, no neon, no modern clothing, no text or labels unless specifically requested.
