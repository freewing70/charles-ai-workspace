# Living Watercolor RPG Skill v1.1

A modular prompt engineering framework designed for generating dynamic, character-consistent watercolor artwork suitable for JRPG concept art and environment design.

## Overview
- **Version**: 1.1
- **Author**: Charles
- **Primary Tools**: Codex, GPT Image, Midjourney v6
- **License**: MIT

## Features
- **Fluid Washes**: Emphasizes transparent pigment bleeding and expressive edge bleeding.
- **Dynamic Character Poses**: Tailored for action stances, spellcasting, and weapon wielding.
- **Atmospheric Perspective**: Environmental cues with mist, paper texture, and granular watercolor pigments.

---

## 繁體中文使用說明

### 1. 核心提示詞結構
本 Skill 採用「主體 + 水彩特徵 + 光影與氛圍 + 構圖與視角」四段式結構：

```
[Character / Scene], in vibrant watercolor RPG concept art style, expressive ink linework, soft translucent pigment washes, rich paint splatters, textured cold-press cotton paper, dynamic action pose, soft rim lighting, masterpiece quality.
```

### 2. Prompt 模組範例

#### 範例 A：戰鬥法師（Battle Mage）
```text
A mystical female battle mage conjuring celestial flames, flowing watercolor robe in indigo and gold hues, fluid ink lineart, translucent pigment gradients, delicate brushstrokes, floating spell runes, dramatic overhead magical lighting, soft watercolor bleeds on edges, highly detailed, 8k resolution.
```

#### 範例 B：綠意盎然的遺跡之城（Ancient Overgrown Ruins）
```text
Ancient fantasy fortress ruins engulfed by vibrant emerald moss and blooming wildflowers, sweeping watercolor landscape, wet-on-wet technique, soft horizon mist, dramatic sunlight filtering through ancient arches, visible paper texture, JRPG concept art, cinematic composition.
```
