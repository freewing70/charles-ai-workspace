# PRODUCT.md — FreewingBiz Product Truth

## Product Identification
- **Primary Brand**: FREEWINGBIZ
- **Secondary Identity**: CHARLES AI WORKSPACE
- **Tagline / Core Philosophy**: AI × Knowledge × Creation × A Better Tomorrow
- **Experience Verbs**: EXPLORE · LEARN · CREATE · SHARE

## Product Purpose & Audience
FreewingBiz is a high-craft personal AI workspace, knowledge hub, project showcase, and interactive presentation center.
- **Target Audience**: AI researchers, software engineers, digital creators, enterprise clients, and learners exploring practical AI tools, skills, and pipelines.
- **Core Functionality**:
  - `Skills`: Curated AI skill modules and prompt workflows.
  - `Tutorials`: Step-by-step technical guides and architectural walkthroughs.
  - `Presentations`: Standalone, interactive HTML5 presentation slide decks.
  - `Projects`: Full-stack and generative AI showcase projects.
  - `Notes`: Research observations and model benchmarks.
  - `Timeline`: Chronological activity and release progress.

## Operating Context
- **Tech Stack**: Astro 5 (Static Generation), Tailwind CSS v4, Fuse.js (Client Search), Lucide Icons.
- **Deployment Target**: Cloudflare Pages Global Edge.
- **Design Philosophy**: Cloud White atmosphere, Deep Navy anchors, Royal & Sky Blue gradients, subtle Sunrise Gold accents. Humanistic, intelligent, open, optimistic.
