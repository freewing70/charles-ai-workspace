# FreewingBiz Brand Specification

## Identity
- Primary brand: FREEWINGBIZ
- Secondary identity: CHARLES AI WORKSPACE
- Brand concept: AI × Knowledge × Creation × A Better Tomorrow
- Experience words: Explore · Learn · Create · Share
- Site role: Personal AI Workspace + Knowledge Base + Project Portfolio + Creative Lab

## Approved symbol
The approved visual direction is the wide **W + Wings** symbol in `../references/freewingbiz-brand-identity.png`.

The silhouette is the key brand property: two large wings spread upward/outward and meet through a central W-shaped construction. It must read as W + Wings before any decorative detail.

Never reinterpret it as a flower, lotus, bud, heart, bird head, leaves, AI brain, circuit mark, or generic icon. `../references/rejected-old-simplified-logo.png` exists only to document a rejected implementation and must never be reused.

## Palette
- Deep Navy: #07172D
- Dark Navy/Text: #0B1B32
- Royal Blue: #155EEF
- Sky Blue: #27A8FF
- Ice Blue: #B9DCFF
- Sunrise Gold: #E8B85C
- Cloud White: #F7F9FC
- Pure White: #FFFFFF

Blue is dominant. Gold is a restrained sunrise reflection/accent only.

## Typography
Prefer Inter or Plus Jakarta Sans. Use clean, editorial typography with generous tracking for brand lockups. Avoid sci-fi display fonts.

## Visual language
Calm, open, premium, intelligent, exploratory, optimistic and human-centered. Use generous whitespace, thin separators, subtle blue-gray borders/shadows, controlled gradients, clean cards and occasional warm highlights. Avoid cyberpunk, neon overload, robots, circuit-board clichés, particle fields, gaming UI and excessive glassmorphism.

## Photography
Mountain/cloud/sunrise imagery establishes atmosphere primarily in the homepage hero and possibly selected major page headers. It must not be repeated behind every section. Background photography used in production must contain no baked-in brand text or logo.
